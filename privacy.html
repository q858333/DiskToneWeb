Privacy Policy

DiskTone is built around local playback. This policy explains what data the app uses and what stays on your device.

Effective date: August 21, 2026 · App version 1.0

Summary: DiskTone does not require an account, does not track you, and does not collect personal data for analytics or advertising. Music stays on your device.

1. Introduction

DiskTone ("we", "our", or "the app") is a local music player for iOS. This Privacy Policy describes how the app handles information when you use it. By using DiskTone, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

DiskTone is designed to operate without collecting personal data. We do not:

  • Require you to create an account or sign in
  • Collect analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics through third-party SDKs
  • Display advertisements
  • Track you across apps or websites
  • Upload your music, listening history, or library metadata to our servers
  • Sync your data to the cloud

Our App Store privacy label reflects this: DiskTone declares that it does not track users and does not collect data types linked to you.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The app stores the following data only on your device to provide its features:

  • Downloaded audio files — saved in the app's private storage when you download from a direct link you provide
  • Download index — title, artist, album, duration, and play timestamps for tracks you downloaded through the app
  • App preferences — settings such as whether cellular downloads are allowed and whether to auto-play after a download completes

This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to us.

4. Access to Your Music Library

With your permission, DiskTone can read your device's system music library to browse and play songs, albums, and artists. The app reads metadata such as title, artist, album, duration, and artwork for display and playback.

DiskTone does not copy system library files to external servers. Playback of system library items uses iOS system APIs. You can revoke library access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Media & Apple Music on your device.

5. Network Usage

DiskTone connects to the internet only when you choose to download audio from a direct HTTP or HTTPS link. The download destination is the URL you enter — we do not operate a fixed backend service for music delivery.

Supported direct download formats are MP3, M4A, and WAV. The app does not parse music streaming services or general web pages. Whether downloads use Wi‑Fi or cellular data is controlled by your in-app setting.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Because DiskTone does not collect personal data on our servers, there is no user database to share.

When you download a file, your device communicates directly with the server hosting that URL. That server is chosen by you, not by DiskTone.

7. Third-Party Components

DiskTone includes open-source libraries used for user interface layout. These components do not receive your music library, download history, or personal identifiers for analytics or advertising.

8. Children's Privacy

DiskTone is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will assist with deletion of any locally stored app data.

9. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You can manage your data as follows:

  • Revoke music library access — in iOS Settings
  • Remove downloaded tracks — delete items within the app
  • Remove all app data — uninstall DiskTone from your device

Uninstalling the app deletes downloaded files, local indexes, and app preferences stored on your device.

10. Data Security

Downloaded files and app data are stored in the app's sandboxed container on iOS, protected by the operating system's access controls. No security measure is perfect, but we do not expose your library to external services operated by us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how DiskTone handles data, contact us at dengcheez@gmail.com.
